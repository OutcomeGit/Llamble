1 · The app

Get Llamble for Windows

The desktop app is the full experience: push-to-talk hotkeys, game-friendly screen sharing with sound, notifications, and lending your GPU to the AI if you want to.

  1. Download and run Llamble-Setup.exe.
  2. Open the invite link you were sent, or type your server's address.
  3. That's it. The app keeps itself up to date.

2 · No install

Use it in your browser

On a phone, Chromebook or any computer, Llamble runs right in the browser: chat, voice and video included. On a phone, choose Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app.

Every Llamble server has its own web address. If someone invited you, just open their invite link; it takes you to the right place.
Open chat.llamble.com

What's inside

Everything you'd expect from a chat app, on hardware you control.

💬

Servers & channels

Text channels, DMs, replies, reactions, GIFs, link previews, search, roles and permissions.

🎙️

Voice, video, screens

High-quality calls, cameras and screen sharing with sound, built on LiveKit.

🦙

Built-in AI

A llama.cpp-powered assistant in your chats. Pick the model; members can lend their gaming GPU when idle.

📁

Files

Drag in images, video and documents up to the size you choose. Everything stays on your server.

🌍

Reach from anywhere

Direct with your own domain, or through your own relay to keep your home IP private. HTTPS set up for you.

🔓

Open source

No accounts with us, no ads, no tracking. Your server, your rules, your data.

3 · Host it yourself

Run your own Llamble server

One container with everything inside: the server, the web app, voice and video, and the AI. It sets up its own storage. Create the admin account and pick an AI model the first time you open it.

# TrueNAS: Apps → Discover Apps → ⋮ → Install via YAML → paste this services: llamble: image: ghcr.io/outcomegit/llamble:latest pull_policy: always restart: unless-stopped ports: - "8090:8080" # app + web client at home - "8443:8443" # HTTPS for remote access (if you turn it on) - "7881:7881" # voice/video (TCP) - "7882:7882/udp" # voice/video volumes: - llamble-data:/data volumes: llamble-data:
# Any Linux machine with Docker: save as docker-compose.yml, then `docker compose up -d` services: llamble: image: ghcr.io/outcomegit/llamble:latest pull_policy: always restart: unless-stopped ports: - "8090:8080" - "8443:8443" - "7881:7881" - "7882:7882/udp" volumes: - llamble-data:/data volumes: llamble-data:

Then open http://YOUR-SERVER-IP:8090 to finish setup. Remote access, AI models, GPU sharing and everything else is configured from inside the app by the admin. The full guide is in the README.
