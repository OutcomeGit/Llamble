# Using Llamble: the plain-English version

Llamble's code is public so you can read it, check what it does with your data, and run it
yourself. It is **source-available, not open source**: it's free to use and self-host, but it
is licensed under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md), which doesn't allow using it
to compete with Llamble.

This page is the licensor's own reading of those terms. Where it says something is allowed, take
it as an extra permission from the licensor on top of the licence.

## Allowed, free

- **Run your own Llamble server** for yourself, your family, your friends, a gaming group, a
  club, a community of any size, or your company and its clients.
- **Let members chip in** to cover what your server costs to run, and sell paid memberships for
  your community through Llamble's own store.
- **Change the code for your own server**, fix bugs, and share your fixes back.
- **Read and audit everything**, write about it, and teach with it.

None of that competes with Llamble, so none of it needs permission.

## Not allowed

- **Selling Llamble hosting**, or running Llamble servers for other people as a business.
- **Rebranding Llamble**, or building a rival chat, voice or streaming app or service from
  its code.
- **Running a rival store** for packs, themes or games inside Llamble.
- **Removing or getting around the protection on store items** (packs, themes, games), or
  sharing them with people who didn't get them from the store. This competes with the
  Llamble Store, and store items have their own terms (below) as well.

If you're not sure whether something you want to do counts as competing, ask first:
<https://llamble.com>. We'd rather say yes than see you guess.

## The name and logo

The licence covers the code only. "Llamble", the llama logo and the app's look are the
licensor's trademarks. A changed copy must not call itself Llamble or look like it's ours.

## Packs, themes and games from the store

Things you get from the Llamble Store are **not** covered by the code licence. They are
licensed to you under the store's terms when you get them, and all rights are reserved. You can use what you
bought on any Llamble server where your store account is linked.

## Other people's code inside Llamble

Llamble includes open-source libraries under their own licences. The full list and texts are in
[client/src/legal/third-party-licenses.txt](client/src/legal/third-party-licenses.txt), and in the app
under **Settings → About**.
