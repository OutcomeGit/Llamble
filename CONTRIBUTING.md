# Contributing to Llamble

Bug reports and ideas are always welcome: open an issue.

## Code contributions

also sells things around it (hosting, the store). So that one outside change can't tie the
project's hands later, code is accepted only on these terms:

> By submitting a contribution (code, text, art or anything else) to this repository, you
> confirm that you wrote it or have the right to submit it, and you grant the licensor of
> Llamble a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use,
> copy, change, sublicense and distribute your contribution under any terms, including terms
> other than the PolyForm Shield License. You keep the copyright in your contribution.

Opening a pull request means you agree to the above. Please say so in the pull request
("I agree to the contribution terms in CONTRIBUTING.md").

Never include keys, passwords, tokens or anything personal in a contribution.
